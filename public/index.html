BEDROCK 101

What is Minecraft Bedrock?

Bedrock Edition is the cross-platform version of Minecraft used on phones, tablets, consoles, Windows and other supported devices. This site focuses on the mobile side of that ecosystem.

Mobile first

Android, iPhone and iPad players can use this hub for guides, servers, content and community.

Add-Ons

Add-Ons can change gameplay and content such as blocks, items, mobs, recipes and more.

Resource Packs

Resource packs replace or add assets such as textures, sounds, models and UI visuals.

Behavior Packs

Behavior packs control gameplay logic such as entity behavior, loot, spawns, recipes and trades.

BEDROCK VS JAVA

Why this site separates Bedrock content

Java mods and Bedrock add-ons are not the same technology. A Java mod cannot simply be installed into Bedrock. This directory therefore labels content by its actual Bedrock category instead of mixing everything together.

COMPETITIVE PVP

Understand the tiers before you chase them.

These are explanations for the tier-list language. The site does not automatically assign tiers.

Apply for tier list
HT1 — High Tier 1

The highest tier used by this community's chosen tier system. It represents players who consistently perform at the top level under the tested ruleset.

HT2 — High Tier 2

A very strong competitive placement. Players are expected to demonstrate consistent mechanics, decision making and matchup awareness.

LT3 — Low Tier 3

A lower placement within Tier 3. The label describes competitive placement, not whether a player is generally “good” or “bad.”

LT4 — Low Tier 4

Players at this placement have demonstrated the required level for the tier-list format but are below the higher competitive bands.

LT5 — Low Tier 5

The lowest band in a five-level tier family. It is still a published competitive placement rather than a claim that someone cannot PvP.

Retest / Review

Rankings can be reviewed through the community process. Staff decide what evidence or testing is appropriate instead of letting the website invent a result.

Google sign-in

If you choose Sign in with Google, the site receives the Google account information needed to identify your account, including your Google subject identifier, email address and basic profile information. Your Google profile photo is used only in the browser UI and is not stored in our database.

Profile information

If you complete your optional community profile, we store your chosen display name and optional YouTube and Discord handles so the community features can use them.
